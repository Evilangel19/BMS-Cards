# Beta log (after 1.2.0)

One line for every beta of the `dev` branch, oldest first: what changed, why, how it was checked. This folder lives on `dev` only; at a release the summary goes to `CHANGELOG.md` and the status to `docs/ROADMAP.md`. The log up to 1.2.0 is in `ROADMAP-to-1.2.md`.

- 1.2.1-beta.1 (2026-11-14): **Fixed: the Map (or another wide page) became narrow** (1300 px, centred) after a visit to the Squadron Briefing. Hiding the Briefing made the page narrow, and the Squadron hides it again each time it draws its tabs (a pilot joins or leaves, a mission arrives) even while another page is on screen. Now the Briefing makes the page narrow only when it was on screen, and only while the Squadron is the page shown. Test: `tools/briefing-test.py` (the Briefing hidden again with the Map on screen: the Map stays wide; it failed before the fix).
- 1.2.1-beta.2 (2026-11-15): **New: Weapons > Target, the target picture** (a Basic Target Graphic in the style of the program; prototypes v1-v3 reviewed by the pilot). The targets come from the DTC (TGT steerpoints 81-99, weapon targets, the target of the attack planner), the target of the flight and "Add target" (the picker of the DTC and the Map), put together by the objective they are on. Background: the plan of the objective (the features of the FED files where they are, symbols by kind, the campaign state), the Map page (lent with its base and layers, framed north up and captured: its corners give the place on the ground) or a pasted screenshot (no scale: arrows at the edge). Filters Recon B/W, IR white-hot, Recon 1980s. "Fill from the datacard": the DMPIs with their STPT, the attack of the attack planner (else the legs of the flight plan), the enemy SAM rings and units near the target. BTG symbols by hand (callout, DMPI, number, box, circle, area, arrow, attack axis, north, scale, text, notes by pen in Caveat). Page 2: the text and two detail pictures. Add card (three cards 2:3: graphic, information, details; free slots first), Put on datacard (the shape of the map screenshot), Save PNG. The pictures and the symbols stay for the session only. Rules in `core/tgtdraw.js` (test `tests/run-tgtdraw.mjs`), page `core/tgtpage.js`, styles `css/style-12.css`. Test: `tools/tgtpage-test.py` (a small DTC and made-up objective files; the frame of the map captured with the target in the middle and 10 NM wide).
- 1.2.1-beta.3 (2026-11-15): **Weapons > Target, the first changes asked by the pilot.** Fixed: the map framed on the Target tab was black (the style of the page canvas, black background and automatic height, also hit the canvases of the map, which covered the base map). A target can be removed from the list (×): its TGT steerpoints and weapon targets are deleted from the DTC (so from the Map), the target of the flight and a route steerpoint are only hidden; each DMPI box has its × too (on screen only). Names of the sub-objectives (a Names button like the one of the Map filters, DMPI or All). The impacts of the stick, the lethal radius and the CBU footprints as on the map of the Weapons page (attack planner). The STPT references in the colours of the Map palette (TGT STPT, weapon target, flight plan). Page buttons without numbers, and the LEVELS menu of the Map lent to the bar of the picture when the background is the map. The plan: the relief of the BMS terrain (HeightMap.raw, a point every 30 m, sun from the north west; water where the height is 0) and small green trees (only a look) in place of the random dots. Put on datacard: first a crop frame in the shape of the datacard or of the MDC (drag, wheel or slider), then Put on datacard. Tests: `tests/run-tgtdraw.mjs` (relief, ground grid), `tools/tgtpage-test.py` (map canvases not black, LEVELS, crop frame, Names, × of a DMPI and of a target deleting the DTC lines).
- 1.2.1-beta.4 (2026-11-15): **Weapons > Target: labels, two sensor filters, Kneeboard cards at one scale.** The labels of the datacard layer are drawn after its symbols and keep off them (a triangle no longer covers a name or the label of a SAM); every DMPI keeps its own label with its STPT (they were joined into "DMPI 1-5" on a plan wider than 4.5 km), points closer than 18 px share one label, a label moved away from its point gets a thin line back to it; the names of all the sub-objectives come after, where they have room. Two filters: UAV FLIR (soft grey, cyan field of view, tracking box and cross, the data of the mission down both sides in place of the blurred parts of the reference picture, a green banner that the pilot can change, CLASSIFIED by default) and Small UAV (cold hazy blue grey, the yellow box with its cross, the sun, the compass arc turning with the run-in heading, the wind of the campaign weather at the target, OFF and VS). Kneeboard cards: a real 2:3 layout (760 x 1140 page pixels to 1600 x 2400) with everything at one scale: the header on three lines, the picture full width, the six DMPI boxes in two rows of three, the text of the information filling the card, the two detail pictures one under the other (the old cards had parts at two scales and much empty space). Test: `tools/tgtpage-test.py` (the Banner field of UAV FLIR, the filters).
- 1.2.1-beta.5 (2026-11-15): **New: Squadron > Missions in order** (for many saved missions). Search (every word in the name, campaign, theater, squadron, callsign, kind, sender or folder), Group by Folders / Campaign / Squadron / Date (one group a month), a hundred missions at a time ("Show 100 more"), groups closed with their counts. Virtual folders (with folders inside folders): New folder, rename, delete (its missions and folders go up one level), missions moved by dragging them onto a folder or by selecting them (Shift: a range) and Move to; several deleted at once. The folders live in `missions/index.json`: the mission folders on the disk never move, so sending, receiving, the data move and Load are unchanged. The index also keeps what the list shows of each mission (read once from its file, read again only when the file changes size), so a long list opens at once; a new mission keeps the squadron of its flight, a received one who sent it. Rules in `core/misindex.js` (test `tests/run-misindex.mjs`), UI test `tools/missions-test.py` (250 missions in a stand-in folder). **Weapons > Target:** the LEVELS menu comes closed when the Map background is chosen (it opens again on the Map page if it was open there); the pointer shows the move cross only over what can be moved (a symbol, the crop frame), a hand over the × of a DMPI box, the draw cross with a drawing tool.
- 1.2.1-beta.6 (2026-11-16): **Weapons > Target.** Fixed: the LEVELS menu lent to the Target tab never went back to the Map page (the map looks for its parts only in its page and in the page that borrows the map, so it could not find the menu any more): it is kept by reference while it is lent, comes back when the tab is left (and when the Map page opens), and the map is made ready for it even when the Map page was never opened. The map background also has Edit points and Map filters (as the horizontal envelope of the Weapons page) and Buildings: the buildings of the objective drawn on the picture of the map, where the objective files put them. The select tool shows a mouse pointer. Add target puts a weapon target into the DTC (as on the Map page), so it is a WPN TGT everywhere; without a DTC or a free slot it stays in the list only. The plan zooms (the wheel on the picture, or − all +; up to x8): it is drawn again for the smaller ground, the trees stand on the ground, the relief is read again, the symbols drawn by hand stay on their ground. A picture dropped on the window of the desktop program (which takes the drops for the files) is the screenshot when the tab is shown. **Squadron > Missions:** dragging works in the desktop program (with the pointer: the program takes the HTML drag and drop of its window for the files); Saved and Received side by side again; New folder makes a folder at the top, ＋ on a folder one inside it; folders with folders inside open and close with their arrow; a folder can be dragged into another one; the head of the list has the box that selects all, the count, Move to, Delete and ✕. Tests: `tools/tgtpage-test.py` (zoom, map buttons, pointer icon), `tools/missions-test.py` (pointer drag, top and inner folders, closing, folder into folder, select all, two columns).
- 1.2.1-beta.7 (2026-11-16): **Squadron > Missions:** All missions and Unfiled are coloured dots (blue, grey ring); the name of a mission has the colour of its campaign (green for the campaign of the oldest mission, then the blue, amber and purple of the HARM tables, and four more), the same in the Campaign groups. The Squadron groups are the online squadron of the player: the one that sent a received mission, else the one chosen when the mission was saved (`osquad` in the mission file, read by `misindex.metaOf`); the older missions are "Personal / no squadron". **Weapons > Target:** the Map background shows the live map of the Map page first (LEVELS, Edit points and Map filters work on it), then 📷 Take picture opens the frame (as wide as the Width chosen, around the middle of the map as it was left) and Capture makes the picture; 🗺 Back to the map takes another one. Clear all is 🗑 Annotations (asks first), Delete selected is 🗑 Selected (off while nothing is chosen); Put on datacard is green; Names on is the green of the chosen settings, as wide as Remove. Notes by pen is Handwritten notes, in the Filter card: it changes the font of all the notes (kept as typed, shown in capitals without it) and of the remarks of the Target information; fixed: the handwriting font was never loaded (`document.fonts.check` says true for a family it does not know), so the fallback of the system was shown. The plan of an airbase draws its runways (the corners of the point files, the end numbers of the landing headers, edges and centre line; the destroyed runway sections as craters) and its taxi paths (runs of the takeoff and taxi points, approximate): `targets.parseAirfield`, `targetdata.airfield`. **Squadron > Briefing:** the tabs of the Squadron page no longer move when the Briefing widens the page. Tests: `run-aip.mjs` (airfield), `run-misindex.mjs` (player squadron), `tools/tgtpage-test.py` (live map, Take picture, Back to the map, bins, green, handwriting font, runway), `tools/missions-test.py` (dots, campaign colours), `tools/briefing-test.py` (tabs still).
- 1.2.1-beta.8 (2026-11-16): **Squadron > Missions:** fixed: the message boxes of the program (alert, confirm) stood at the bottom of the window: the groups of the list had the class `msg` of the boxes (with its margin), now `msgrpb`. The dots of the campaigns are round (the arrow width of the group head squeezed them); the first group opens by itself only when the grouping is chosen, so all the groups can be closed. **Weapons > Target:** fixed: a level chosen in the LEVELS menu lent to the tab did nothing (the map looked for the parts of the menu only in its page and in the page that borrows the map: `$` of `mapview` looks in the lent menu too). Tests: `tools/missions-test.py` (box in the middle, all groups closed, round dots), `tools/tgtpage-test.py` (a level chosen, zoom locked and back in the frame, dark green).
- 1.2.1-beta.9 (2026-11-16): **Weapons > Target, the layout.** Three columns as the Pop-up tab (330 px, the picture as wide as the horizontal envelope, 400 px): Target, Background and From the datacard on the left, Filter and Page on the right. - 1.2.1-beta.10 (2026-11-16): **Weapons > Target.** No Edit points: the map lent to the tab never edits its points (`mapV.attach(el, true)`), the tab is for drawing. The plan is called Synthetic. It turns: ↺ 15°, N up, ↻ 15° and Attack up (the attack heading at the top) in the Background card; the plan is drawn again turned (`tgtdraw.rotBox`, `planBox(..., deg)`: no empty corners), the symbols drawn by hand stay on their ground (`replan`, also used by the zoom, whose point under the pointer stays under it on a turned plan), the buildings stand square to the grid of the ground, the runway numbers read along their true heading. The picture of the map turns in its frame (Angle and +90°, as on the Map page): it comes straightened, its corners turned on the ground. On a turned picture the North symbol keeps to the true north (`northOn`) and the RUN-IN of the attack axis is a heading on the ground. Buildings is a button of the tool bar (shown on the picture of the map only). Filters: Recon B/W is IR white-hot, IR white-hot is IR black-hot, Small UAV is Small drone, UAV FLIR is black hot (BLK HOT on its display); the new Targeting pod (white hot, hard contrast, the bloom of the bright things, dark edges) has the display of a pod over it: the black bands, the heading tape, POINT / IR WHT, ACFT with speed and altitude, the target with its coordinates, bearing and elevation, the field of view, the cross, the elevation scale, SEL and the clock. Tests: `tools/tgtpage-test.py` (no Edit points, turn, N up, frame at 90°, eight filters).
